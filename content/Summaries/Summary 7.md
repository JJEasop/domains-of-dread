# Summary 7

Journey Preparations and Inclement Weather Travel

Following the acquisition of two Vardo wagons and two draft horses from the Vistani, the party organized their travel roster and commenced their journey toward Immol:

* Caravan Organization & Seating Arrangements:
   * Wagon 1: Driven by Ireena, accompanied by Lucia and Michael positioned at the front cab.
   * Wagon 2: Driven by Father Merrick, transporting Gertrude (resting inside), Levi, and the two junior clerics, Father Andre and Father David (collectively dubbed the "Tweedle twins").
   * Health Updates: Gertrude’s condition improved substantially after leaving continuous toxicity; she exhibited restored color, enhanced cognition, and responsive movement. Irina displayed complete physical recovery.


* Itinerary & Route Strategy:
   * The journey to Immol was mapped as a 3-day transit. Merrick strongly advocated against roadside camping due to nocturnal perils, establishing intermediate resting stops at a crossroads inn on the first evening and a southern village on the second.


* Road Conditions & Travel Encounters:
   * Deteriorating Weather: Initial morning drizzles intensified into a driving, visibility-impairing storm, churning the unpaved roads into thick mud that contrasted unfavorably with engineered Roman highways.
   * Vistani Scouts: The party encountered three scouts on foot heading north to replace Jordy's departing caravan, confirming that caravan movements are fluid rather than operating on fixed schedules.
   * Passing Farmers: Encountered rural workers moving in the opposite direction wearing oiled, weather-treated attire.
   * Forest Grave: Noticed a freshly dug roadside grave near the tree line marked by a modest wooden cross-like monument.



Roadside Ambush and Wolf Pack Combat

Late in the afternoon, the party heard desperate cries for assistance near a bend in the road and engaged a pack of wolves assailing a stranded cart:

* Surprise Round Engagements:
   * Linnorden: Cast Silent Image, cloaking the two moving Vardos in the illusion of two massive, 20-foot-long dire megafauna bears charging forward.
   * Pip: Cast Magic Missile from long range, inflicting 4 points of force damage on the nearest wolf.
   * Lucia: Bestowed Bless across all party members, securing a +1 morale bonus to attack rolls and fear saves.
   * Father Merrick: Cast Bull's Strength on Linnorden (+4 strength bonus).


* Tactical Combat Execution:
   * Initial Casualties: A male traveler on the ground was examined by Lucia via an 18 Medicine check and confirmed deceased; a defending woman on the wagon seat was struck down by a wolf bite.
   * Crowd Control & Retaliation: Linnorden executed Hypnotic Pattern (DC 16), dazzling and fascinating both active wolves with shimmering light ribbons and auditory accompaniment.
   * Martial Resolution: Ireena leaped from the wagon and delivered a critical rapier strike dealing 16 damage, instantly felling the first wolf. Michael charged the second wolf with his greatsword, inflicting 14 damage and finishing the beast.
   * Final Strike: Linnorden deployed Mage Hand to distract the remaining wolf; Lucia subsequently landed a morningstar strike, and Michael struck the final blow down the beast's spine.
   * Medical Aid: Lucia administered Cure Light Wounds (restoring 11 hit points) to the fallen woman, who endured significant system shock from the rapid healing surge.


* Survivor Identification & Wagon Recovery:
   * The survivor identified herself as Nastazya from the northern village of Rottermegen, traveling to sell grain in Immol.
   * Her draft horse had fled during the wolf assault. The party loaded the deceased brother into her wagon, escorted Nastazya on foot to the nearby inn, and dispatched Pip alongside Father Andre and Father David with a horse to retrieve the abandoned cargo wagon.



Arrival, Lodging Arrangements, and Mind Scrying at Curley Cross Inn

The party arrived at the fortified Curly Cross Inn as the storm elevated into a full thunderous squall:

* Inn Description & Architecture:
   * A two-story structure featuring a thatched roof, ground-floor glass windows, second-story security shutters, and an enclosing 10-foot wooden defensive palisade with an open gate. Warning bells were affixed to the establishment’s exterior sign and several windows.


* Lodging & Commercial Terms:
   * Michael negotiated with the innkeeper, securing the final three available accommodations for a lump sum of 5 gold pieces covering lodging, stabling, and hot meals:
      * Room 1: Four single bunk beds (assigned to Pip, Michael, Linnorden, and Levi).
      * Room 2: Two queen beds (allocated to Father Merrick, Father Andre, and Father David).
      * Room 3: One queen and two twin beds (assigned to Lucia, Ireena, Gertrude, and Nastazya).


* Mind Scrying & Intelligence Assessment (Detect Thoughts):
   * Linnorden systematically conducted Detect Thoughts across the common room, mapping intellectual capabilities and surface motives:
      * Nastazya: Recorded an exceptionally high Intelligence score of 18; her surface thoughts remained unreadable.
      * The Scholar: Stationed in the corner covered in research papers and scrolls; scored an Intelligence of 17 and successfully passed his Will save against mind reading.
      * Innkeeper & Staff: The innkeeper's active surface thought was revealed as "I wonder what time it's going to start," while the isolated stable boy thought about being unusually ordered to sleep exclusively in the stable for the night.
      * Gertrude: Confined to an upstairs bed, looping an ominous mental lullaby ("I left my baby lying here to go and gather berries").
      * Patrons: Brothers debating fishing disruptions and unexplained disappearances in Stillbrook; a maid mentally cataloging sewing chores accompanied by an attentive bodyguard.



Threat Assessment, Monster Lore Analysis, and Nighttime Preparations

Reconvening privately over dinner, the party synthesized their tactical intelligence, magical detections, and recovered lore to prepare for imminent threats:

* Alignment Detections (Detect Evil):
   * Lucia performed divine alignment scans, detecting no presence of uppercase supernatural evil, but registering lowercase moral evil radiating from four individuals: the scholarly noble in the corner, his armed bodyguard, the common-room bard, and Nastazya herself.
   * Father Merrick observed that the physical wounds on Nastazya's deceased "brother" were cut far too cleanly to have originated from wolf claws or teeth, raising grave doubts about her true identity.


* Recovered Beast Hunter Log:
   * While inspecting Nastazya's wagon, Pip discovered a concealed hunter’s journal cataloging dangerous beasts. The log detailed encounters with magical lycanthropes, emphasizing that werewolves are destructive magical entities requiring pure silver weapons to pierce their defenses.


* Defensive Protocols & Readiness:
   * The party established internal security rotations and active night-watch shifts within each bedchamber, noting that the lunar cycle sat on the threshold of a full moon.
   * Combat resources were reviewed, including Michael's silver blade, a single silver crossbow bolt, and backup plans to utilize heated metal implements if an assault materialized.
