# Summary 8

Tavern Interactions, Scholar Discourse, and Poison Investigation
The party’s dinner was pending when they resumed play. Merrick offered to help Nastasia prepare the body for burial and keep watch over her. Lucia joined him, partly to provide support and partly because the party was wary of leaving anyone alone with Nastasia. Meanwhile, Linnordin approached the scholar sitting in the corner, with Pip accompanying them.
Linnordin opened the conversation in Draconic. The scholar, later identified as Ozrin, was delighted to meet someone who spoke what he recognized as the Halan and asked where Linnordin had learned it. Linnordin explained that they were from the Forest of Arantellion and had arrived in Barovia after becoming lost in the mists. Ozrin recognized them as “Mist Walkers” and eagerly asked questions.
Ozrin said he was from Velaki and came from a family with a long academic history. He was traveling to Emol at the invitation of Burgemeister Theodorus, to study at the archives there. Ozrin described the archive as divided into sections requiring separate permission to access; he had previously visited only its public library and had recently been invited to conduct research. He was enthusiastic about his first invitation.
Linnordin offered Ozrin a spell from their spellbook in exchange for learning one of his. Ozrin had limited material to offer and accepted Linnordin’s suggestion to copy Mage Armor. While they spoke, Linnordin asked about Barovia’s general condition and rumors of dangerous creatures appearing around the full moon. Ozrin said that violence and warnings against going outside after dark were familiar parts of life there, but dismissed werewolf stories as possibly nonsense. Linnordin’s Sense Motive check indicated that Ozrin was lying about that point.
Linnordin also asked about a sketch of a waystone. Ozrin said he had seen one north of Velaki, on the far side of Lake Zarevich, but could not interpret its language or determine its purpose. He suggested that the subject might interest Theodorus and could help Linnordin gain access to the archives. Ozrin said he had considered bypassing Stillbrook because of the reports of missing people, though his bodyguard appeared unhappy when Ozrin mentioned camping in the wilderness. Linnordin offered to ask the party whether Ozrin could travel with them; no decision was made. Pip, meanwhile, responded to Ozrin’s academic name-dropping with references to his own family history and the There and Back Again folio.
In the common room, Lucia noticed that the innkeepers were watching the party closely. The employee behind the bar repeatedly looked toward them and seemed frightened, though Lucia could not tell that he was specifically afraid of the party. The group speculated that their presence might turn a local incident into a larger conflict. Michael also jokingly wondered whether the innkeeper might be considering insurance fraud. Neither explanation was confirmed.
The innkeeper’s wife delivered drinks to Michael and Irina and left a small note reading “Do not drink.” The party refrained from drinking the dark red wine. Linnordin dropped a copper coin into one mug; the liquid bubbled slightly. Pennig, Linnordin’s familiar, compared that coin with an untreated copper piece and detected a “bitter plant” smell on the coin that had been in the wine. The conversation suggested that the substance was a sedative, but the exact ingredient was not identified. Pennig later reported smelling the same bitter plant in the kitchen.
The innkeeper’s wife served the food separately and made pointed eye contact with Michael, nodding encouragingly. The party understood this as a signal that the food was safe and ate dinner. The innkeeper later took an unidentified item from the tray he carried upstairs, after glancing toward Merrick. The significance of the item was not established.
Linnordin successfully cast Message, creating a discreet communication link among the party members within the spell’s limits. The party used it to share observations without alerting the other guests.
Nighttime Strategy, Room Reallocations, and Undead Encounter
The inn had only three rooms available. The party rearranged sleeping plans so Lucia, Michael, Pip, Linnordin, and Irina would share one room, with Pip and Linnordin sharing a bed; Linnordin could meditate at the foot of it. The clerics and Gertrude would use another room, with Gertrude in her litter. This arrangement also kept Lucia from having to sleep in the same room as Nastasia and the body.
Lucia had prepared Augury and asked whether spending the night next to Nastasia by herself would bring good or bad results. The answer was Wheel and Woe, indicating both good and bad outcomes. A Religion check of 23 led Lucia to sense that something was off with the spell; she received no explanation of the irregularity.
Michael took the first watch, with Linnordin to take the remaining watch after completing four hours of meditation. During Michael’s watch, he spotted a shadow moving down the hallway and movement in the light beneath the door. He heard nothing at the time. Linnordin later heard a crash, followed by footsteps running toward the party’s room and urgent knocking.
Nastasia appeared at the door, looking panicked, and said Wilhelm was not dead. Linnordin sensed that she seemed both panicked and calculating. The party warned one another through Message, then went toward the room where Wilhelm’s body had been prepared. Pip stayed behind initially, while Irina was already awake and armed.
In the room, the party found Wilhelm risen as an undead ghast. It resembled the undead they had fought in the Durst House, but its stench was overwhelming. Lucia moved into position and used her Sun Domain’s Greater Turning. She rolled 16, adding her cleric level for a total of 22; the ghast was destroyed and turned to dust. Nastasia appeared frightened but not deeply distressed by the loss of the body.
Lucia and Linnordin helped collect the dust into a waterskin using Mage Hand, which Linnordin cast for the day. Nastasia said the remains would be easier to transport now, though the party had not determined where they would ultimately be taken. Earlier, Lucia had considered using Gentle Repose to preserve Wilhelm’s body for a journey of about a week to Rotermegan; that plan became moot when the body was destroyed.
The party also checked on the clerics. They were still deeply asleep and difficult to rouse, apparently affected by the sedative, though Michael could not determine whether Merrick was drugged or simply very tired. Gertrude was awake in the room, quietly playing cat’s cradle with a thread pulled from her dress. She became withdrawn when Lucia asked what she last remembered. When Lucia asked who had sent her, Gertrude pointed at Father Andre. Lucia noticed that Gertrude’s gown was shredded and that she had old lines or scars on her wrists. Merrick said Gertrude did not appear to be in immediate danger but recommended that someone remain awake with her at all times.
Covert Surveillance, Nastasia’s Departure, and the Scholar’s Return
After the ghast was destroyed, Linnordin used Invisibility to observe Nastasia while the others left the room. Once alone, Nastasia stopped acting frightened. She appeared confused and thoughtful, then began putting the room back in order and reached into the pile of dust. Linnordin later returned visibly and offered to help gather the remains respectfully.
During their conversation, Linnordin asked Nastasia about Wilhelm and tried to lead her into contradicting herself. Nastasia noticed the attempt and said Linnordin did not need to be so suspicious. Linnordin replied, “I’ve lived too long among humans to fully trust them all.” Nastasia said she had not caused Wilhelm’s transformation. She also remarked that the party was more interesting than she had originally been told, confirming that she had been sent to watch them. She then said she did not want to overstay her welcome and left.
Earlier, Nastasia had described a younger brother as cheerful and helpful. Linnordin concluded that the details described a real person, but not Wilhelm. The identity of that person and the nature of Nastasia’s relationship to him remained unresolved.
Michael and Irina checked the inn’s perimeter and the stable boy, who answered their door sleepy but unharmed. They also heard the innkeeper and his wife arguing quietly in the kitchen. The wife said she could not, in good conscience, let “it” happen; the innkeeper urged her to think about the children, and she began to weep. The matter they were arguing about was not identified, and its connection to the party or Nastasia was not established.
Nastasia walked directly out of the inn into the storm. During a lightning flash, she saw Michael and Irina, paused, and curtsied. When lightning flashed again, she had vanished. Ozrin entered the yard from the road at that same moment and looked toward the place where she had been. Linnordin searched the rumpled pillow in the room where Nastasia had slept and found a hair; its color was not confirmed.
Ozrin then remarked that the party was supposed to be asleep. The session ended before the party responded or clarified what he was doing outside.
