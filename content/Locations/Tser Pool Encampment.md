# Tser Pool Encampment

## Overview
A lakeside **Vistani** camp reached by the northward road from the [[Gallows Crossroads]], a few hours from [[Barovia]]. Tents and wagons, large herds of **draft horses and donkeys** (no warhorses), a few swift messenger mounts kept apart, and busy craftwork everywhere.

## Arrival
The party arrived in late afternoon or early evening. Children spotted them, and an **elder captain** (pipe, walking stick; unnamed) invited them to dine and promised an introduction to his **sister, the caravan's elder**. Horse-trading and wagon selection start the next day.

## Vistani Etiquette
- Accept gifts, say "thank you," and **do not pay** for gifts (unlike Fey etiquette).
- Don't abuse their hospitality, and don't mention knowing the Barovia innkeeper.
- There is no institutional animosity between the Vistani and the Church of the Morning Lord, though individual clergy may clash.

## Goals
Buy a wagon and draft animals for the journey south.

## Summary 6: Hospitality, Tales and the Tarokka
### The Camp
- A **circle of vardos** with tents outside it. A breakdown clay bread oven, herb drying racks, laundry lines and craft stations.
- Dinner: spiced **deer stew**, a bird-based rice pilaf and warm bread, on rugs around a central fire.
- Elders smoke pipes; craftsmen whittle and weave baskets; groups play dice and board games. Children are lively and curious, **unlike Barovia's "dead-eyed" townsfolk**. A toddler fixated on Lucia's holy symbol.
- One pipe was made of the same wood as [[Madam Eva]]'s: fine-grained, very dark, reddish, with a metallic glimmer.
- Instruments: violins, a viola, lutes and a **modern-style acoustic guitar**. No bagpipes, but a chanter-like drone was heard.

### People
- [[Jordi]], the captain (the elder captain who welcomed the party on arrival), [[Vadoma]], the elder, and [[Marcella (Vistani Storyteller)|Marcella]], the storyteller.

### Events
- [[Summary 6]]: Jordi confirmed the ghost procession from [[Barovia]] climbs past the waterfall to [[Castle Ravenloft]].
- Marcella told the tales of [[Gabriella (Vistani Legend)|Gabriella]] and [[Malocchio]], of [[Sasha (Monster Hunter Priest)|Sasha]], and of [[Vistan]] and [[Caldresh]].
- Vadoma gave a private Tarokka reading: see [[Tarokka Reading (Summary 6)]].
- Long rest. One nightmare: the deck of a **sinking galley**, loved ones bound to the masts.
- Morning: Linnorden scribed *Detect Thoughts* and *Obscuring Mist*.

### The Trade
- **Bought:** **two vardos**, a covered wagon (with fitted cover) and **two draft horses**.
- **Paid:** wine, a tent, torches (incl. an ever-burning torch), a climber's kit, two pounds of nails and Pip's unopened **keg of Longbottom leaf**. The party **kept its three healing potions**; the silver jewelry box was refused and returned.
- A DC 22 search of the vardo found a **hidden book under a bench seat**.

### Vistani Terms
- **Rauni:** elder woman, caravan leader.
- **Mortu:** a Vistani who has settled and no longer travels.
- **Dukkar:** a Vistani man born with the sight who hunts Vistani.
