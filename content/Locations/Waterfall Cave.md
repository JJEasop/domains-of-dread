# Waterfall Cave

## Overview
A damp passage behind the waterfall, entered after the party found [[Father Miroslav]]'s body. [[Merrick]] asked them to investigate it while he returned to the [[Barovia Monastery]].

## Features
- A **5-foot-wide** passage with an unusually high **8-foot ceiling**, roughly carved and clearly unnatural.
- **Quartz-like crystals** in the walls give off a steady pale white glow. Magic analysis showed they are *not* naturally phosphorescent. A chisel bounced off them, so no sample could be taken.
- The tunnel slopes down in gentle curves and widens to about 15 feet before opening onto a ledge above the [[Rope Bridge Cavern]].

## Leads To
- [[Rope Bridge Cavern]], then the [[Ancient Temple]] and [[Ophelia's Residence]].
