# Crypt

## Overview
Site of the party's earlier crisis management as dawn approached.

## Features
- Poison-tainted **rose perfume**.
- Corpses.
- A **hidden altar**.
- A **broken circle**, which the party weighed as possibly not coincidence.

## Events
- The party sourced **powdered silver** (from stolen silverware) and made **holy water** to deal with the aftermath, then withdrew to the [[Sanatorium]].
