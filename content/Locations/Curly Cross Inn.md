# Curly Cross Inn

> Spelling confirmed as **Curly Cross**. "Curley Cross" from [[Summary 7]] is kept as an alias.

## Overview
A **fortified crossroads inn** on the [[Road to Immol]], about a day's travel south of the [[Tser Pool Encampment]]. [[Merrick]] chose it as the first night's stop so the party would not camp by the road.

## Description
- Two stories, **thatched roof**. **Glass windows** on the ground floor; **security shutters** on the second.
- Enclosed by a **10-foot wooden palisade** with an open gate.
- **Warning bells** hang from the exterior sign and several windows.

## People
- [[Innkeeper (Curly Cross Inn)|The innkeeper]] and the [[Stable Boy (Curly Cross Inn)|stable boy]].
- [[Ozrin|A scholarly noble]] in the corner, with his [[Scholar's Bodyguard (Curly Cross Inn)|bodyguard]].
- [[Bard (Curly Cross Inn)|A bard]] in the common room.
- [[Fishing Brothers (Curly Cross Inn)|Two brothers]].
- [[Maid (Curly Cross Inn)|A maid]] with [[Maid's Bodyguard (Curly Cross Inn)|her own bodyguard]], who is not the scholar's.
- [[Nastazya]], brought in by the party.

## Events
### [[Summary 7]]: A Stormy Night
- The party arrived as the storm became a **thunderous squall**, escorting [[Nastazya]] on foot. Pip, [[Andre]] and [[David]] went back with a horse for her wagon.
- Michael got the **last three rooms** for **5 gp total** (lodging, stabling and hot meals):
  - **Room 1** (four bunks): Pip, Michael, Linnorden, [[Levi]].
  - **Room 2** (two queens): [[Merrick]], [[Andre]], [[David]].
  - **Room 3** (one queen, two twins): Lucia, [[Ireena Kolyana|Ireena]], [[Gertrude (Barovia Monastery Asylum)|Gertrude]], [[Nastazya]].
- Linnorden cast *Detect Thoughts* across the common room:
  - [[Nastazya]]: **Int 18**, thoughts unreadable.
  - [[Ozrin|The scholar]]: **Int 17**, **passed his save**.
  - [[Innkeeper (Curly Cross Inn)|Innkeeper]]: **"I wonder what time it's going to start."**
  - [[Stable Boy (Curly Cross Inn)|Stable boy]]: was unusually **ordered to sleep in the stable** tonight.
  - [[Gertrude (Barovia Monastery Asylum)|Gertrude]] (upstairs): looping a lullaby, "I left my baby lying here to go and gather berries."
  - [[Fishing Brothers (Curly Cross Inn)|Brothers]]: fishing troubles and **disappearances in [[Stillbrook]]**.
  - [[Maid (Curly Cross Inn)|A maid]]: sewing chores; [[Maid's Bodyguard (Curly Cross Inn)|an attentive bodyguard]] with her.
- Lucia's *Detect Evil*: **no supernatural Evil**, but **ordinary evil** in four people: the scholar, his bodyguard, the bard and Nastazya.
- **Full moon is on the threshold.** The party set watches in each room. Silver on hand: Michael's **silver blade** and **one silver crossbow bolt**, plus heated metal as a fallback.

### [[Summary 8]]: Drugged Wine and a Ghast
- With dinner pending, [[Merrick]] and Lucia stayed with [[Nastazya]] while she prepared [[Wilhelm]]'s body for burial.
- Linnorden and Pip spoke with the scholar, now named [[Ozrin]], in **Draconic**. He is from [[Vallaki]] and bound for the [[Immol]] archive.
- The innkeepers **watched the party closely**; the [[Innkeeper (Curly Cross Inn)|innkeeper]] looked frightened behind the bar.
- The [[Innkeeper's Wife (Curly Cross Inn)|innkeeper's wife]] slipped Michael and Ireena a note, **"Do not drink"**. The dark red wine was **drugged with a sedative** ("bitter plant" smell, per [[Pennig]]; the same smell is in the **kitchen**). She signalled the food was safe.
- The [[Innkeeper (Curly Cross Inn)|innkeeper]] glanced at [[Merrick]], then took an item from a tray going upstairs.
- Linnorden linked the party with *Message*.
- **New rooms:** Lucia, Michael, Pip, Linnorden and Ireena in one; the clerics and [[Gertrude (Barovia Monastery Asylum)|Gertrude]] (in her litter) in another; Nastazya and the body in the third.
- Michael's watch: a **shadow moving down the hallway** and movement in the light under the door.
- Later a **crash**, running footsteps and knocking: Nastazya said Wilhelm was not dead. He had **risen as a ghast**; Lucia's Greater Turning **reduced him to dust**.
- The clerics were **sedated and hard to rouse**. Gertrude was awake and pointed at [[Andre]] when asked who sent her.
- In the kitchen the innkeepers argued: she could not let **"it"** happen; he said **think about the children**.
- Nastazya **walked out into the storm** and vanished between lightning flashes. Ozrin came in from the road at that moment and remarked that the party was **"supposed to be asleep."**
