# Mountain Road

## Overview
The route down from the [[Barovia Monastery]] to [[Barovia]], dangerous at night.

## Events: Wolf Ambush
About three hours down, at dusk, a pack of **10 wolves** (including two large ones) ambushed the evacuation column. The party spotted them early and won through tactics and illusions, and the wolves fled once both large wolves ("alphas") were dead.

- Lenorden's *Hypnotic Pattern* fascinated three wolves, and *Silent Image* with *Ghost Sound* (a falling tree and a rumbling avalanche) scared others off.
- Pip used *Magic Missile* and a Nine of Swords (The Torturer) auto-crit sneak attack for 41 damage, blinding one large wolf in one eye.
- Michael held the line and landed a 32-damage greatsword crit on the first large wolf.
- [[Merrick]] cast *Bless*, *Prayer* and *Bear's Endurance*, and killed the second large wolf with *Inflict Wounds*.
- [[David]] enthralled two wolves. [[Maxim]] used a sling, [[Alexei]] shielded allies, [[Levi]] observed, and [[Andre]] held a torch.
- Pip and Lucia skinned a large wolf (Survival 13), yielding enough fur for a hobbit-sized cloak.
