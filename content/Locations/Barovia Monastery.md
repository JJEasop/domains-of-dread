# Barovia Monastery

## Overview
Home of the clergy ([[Father Miroslav]], [[Merrick]], the [[High Priestess]]). The cliffside stairs beyond the [[Whistling Door Tunnel]] lead to a spot **opposite Barovia and the monastery**, so it sits across the gorge from there.

## Inhabitants
- [[Merrick]]: returned here to manage internal threats.
- [[Penning]]: shadowing Merrick, unseen.
- [[Father Miroslav]]: formerly; now dead, body to be retrieved.

## Events
- Merrick sends a signed letter to the [[High Priestess]] and arranges to retrieve Miroslav's body.

## Status: Evacuated
- [[Merrick]] **closed the monastery temporarily** and led the evacuation toward [[Zidenberg]] via [[Barovia]].
- The **library is locked**, with its priceless books left behind to be fetched later by runner.
- [[Mara Blackford]]'s cairn here is empty, and the grave was disturbed.
- The abbot [[Andre]] and [[David]] were defrocked and taken into custody.
- The party left a letter for [[Ophelia]] in forged handwriting.

## Hooks
- Internal threats (per Merrick). 💡 *Suggested:* someone inside may be tied to Miroslav's death.
