# Road to Immol

## Overview
The unpaved road south from the [[Tser Pool Encampment]] to [[Immol]], a **3-day journey** by wagon. [[Merrick]] advised against camping by the road at night, so the party planned stops at the [[Curly Cross Inn]] (night one) and a **southern village** (night two).

## Events
### [[Summary 7]]: Day One
- Morning drizzle turned into a **driving storm**. The road became **thick mud**, a poor match for engineered Roman highways.
- **Three Vistani scouts** on foot, heading north to replace [[Jordi]]'s departing caravan. Caravan movements are **fluid, not on a fixed schedule**.
- **Farmers** heading the other way in oiled, weatherproofed clothes.
- A **freshly dug grave** near the tree line, marked with a small wooden cross-like monument.

### Wolf Ambush at the Bend
Late afternoon, cries for help near a bend: wolves attacking a stranded cart.
- **Surprise round:** Linnorden's *Silent Image* made the two vardos look like **two 20-foot dire bears** charging. Pip's *Magic Missile* hit for 4. Lucia cast *Bless*. [[Merrick]] cast *Bull's Strength* on Linnorden.
- A man on the ground was already **dead** (Lucia, Medicine 18): [[Nastazya's Brother]]. A woman on the wagon seat, [[Nastazya]], fell to a wolf bite.
- Linnorden's *Hypnotic Pattern* (DC 16) fascinated both active wolves.
- [[Ireena Kolyana|Ireena]] leapt down and killed the first wolf with a **16-damage rapier crit**. Michael's greatsword (14) finished the second.
- Linnorden used *Mage Hand* to distract the last wolf; Lucia hit it with her morningstar and Michael struck it down through the spine.
- Lucia's *Cure Light Wounds* (11 hp) revived Nastazya, who went into **shock** from the sudden healing.
- Her **draft horse had bolted**. The party loaded the body onto her wagon and walked her to the [[Curly Cross Inn]]; Pip, [[Andre]] and [[David]] took a horse back for the wagon.
