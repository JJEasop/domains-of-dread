# Monolith Site

## Overview
A new monolith ringed by smaller stones, at the top of the [[Cliffside Stairs]].

## Features
- Many of the carved images, **especially those of hairy creatures**, have been **deliberately chiseled away**.
- The party compared it with rubbings and notes from a previous monolith.

## Visitors
- [[Merrick]] was fascinated by it.

## Hooks
- 💡 *Suggested:* Who erased the hairy creatures, and why? Possible link to the [[Waterfall Cave]].
