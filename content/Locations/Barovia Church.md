# Barovia Church

## Overview
The consecrated church of [[Barovia]]. It was abandoned when the party arrived, but still consecrated.

## Events
- [[Ismark]]'s note under its door pleaded for help for [[Ireena]].
- Ireena was moved here under **protective wards**. [[Merrick]] made holy water and performed a restoration ritual.
- At midnight the **ghost procession** rose again, led this time by a **spectral rider** who saluted the party. The horse's barding bore a crest matching the **Von Holtz** carriage.
- The wards repelled [[The Vampire]] twice with an electrical zap.
- Windows were avoided during the vigil, per Merrick.

## Inhabitants
- [[Olaf]], [[Alexei]] and [[Maxim]] remain to assist at the church.
