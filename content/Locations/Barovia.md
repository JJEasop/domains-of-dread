# Barovia

## Overview
The village at the foot of the mountain below the [[Barovia Monastery]]. The [[Cliffside Stairs]] exit opposite it. The Bürgermeister of Barovia is the contact for correspondence with [[Ophelia]].

## Events
- The evacuation caravan from the monastery arrived here safely after the wolf ambush on the [[Mountain Road]].

## Current Visitors
- [[Merrick]], [[Andre]] and [[David]] (in custody), [[Gertrude (Barovia Monastery Asylum)|Gertrude]], [[Olaf]], [[Levi]], [[Maxim]], [[Alexei]]

## Places and People
- [[Barovia Church]], [[Barovia Churchyard]], the Burgermeister's estate
- [[Billdrath]] (Mercantile), [[Geralt]] (tailor), [[Doctor Donovich]], a blacksmith (sold the party two pounds of nails for 1 gp) and an innkeeper (both unnamed)
- Leaders: [[Ismark Kolyanovich]]; the late [[Burgermeister of Barovia]]

## Events
- A vampire is stalking the town: see [[The Vampire]], [[Ireena Kolyana]].
- The Burgermeister was murdered and buried in the family mausoleum.
- The 787 grave in the churchyard was found exhumed: [[Kolyan Vasilyev]].
- The party departed north to the [[Gallows Crossroads]] and [[Tser Pool Encampment]].

## Lore
- [[Summary 6]]: [[Jordi]] says the nightly ghost procession from Barovia climbs past the waterfall toward [[Castle Ravenloft]].
- Vistani tale: a burgomaster sent the Vistani [[Petia]] to the gallows for loving his daughter [[Elena (Burgomaster's Daughter)|Elena]]; the caravan then **avoided Barovia for two generations**. Later the burgomaster's home burned, punishment from **the Count** for cheating taxes. See [[Sasha (Monster Hunter Priest)]].
- The party noted the Vistani children were lively, unlike Barovia's "dead-eyed" townsfolk.

## Hooks
- A **tax collector** visited three days before the murder.
- Barovia's militia is being organized by [[Ismark Kolyanovich]].
