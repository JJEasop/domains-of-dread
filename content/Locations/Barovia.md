# Barovia

## Overview
The village at the foot of the mountain below the [[Barovia Monastery]]. The [[Cliffside Stairs]] exit opposite it. The Bürgermeister of Barovia is the contact for correspondence with [[Ophelia]].

## Events
- The evacuation caravan from the monastery arrived here safely after the wolf ambush on the [[Mountain Road]].
