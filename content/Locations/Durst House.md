# Durst House

## Overview

Manse of the Durst Family. Where the party met the ghosts of [[Rosavalda Durst]] and [[Thornboldt Durst]]. We met [[Margret]] on the third floor of the house. Margret, also a ghost, sacrificed herself to stop an animated suit of armor after entreating the party to save [[Walter Durst]]. On the fourth floor we discovered the bodies of Rose and Thorn. Rose and Thorn appeared again to the party where they revealed they had starved to death.

After that conversation the front door to the house locked. 

After finding their way to the basement the party found and fought [[Mrs. Durst]], and [[Mr. Durst]]. Discovered that Mr. Durst had committed suicide, and Mrs. Durst had a spell book, the spells written in infernal, and blood. At the very bottom of the basement there was discovered a sacrificial chamber, Walter had been placed on the alter ready for sacrifice. Walter was not living, made of human material but not a human baby. Flesh that had been reconstituted into a baby. 

The roof began collapsing.
