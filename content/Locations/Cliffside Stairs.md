# Cliffside Stairs

## Overview
Stairs on the cliff face where the [[Whistling Door Tunnel]] exits, opposite Barovia and the [[Barovia Monastery]]. Damp stone, wind, and mountain orientation made the climb tricky. They lead up to the [[Monolith Site]].
