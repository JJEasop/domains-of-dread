# Rope Bridge Cavern

## Overview
A vast cavern with a deep, dark gorge, reached from the ledge at the end of the [[Waterfall Cave]] passage.

## Features
- A **50-foot rope bridge**: "very old" and "a little frayed," creaking and swaying as the party crossed. It caused real fear and anxiety.
- Strange **echoes**: one character heard a second, different-pitched voice mimicking their words.

## Leads To
- A round, smooth-walled room with a spiral staircase down to the [[Ancient Temple]].
