# Ophelia's Residence

## Overview
The part of the [[Ancient Temple]] that [[Ophelia]] calls her home. The party first heard a sad, quiet lullaby down a hallway lined with small bedrooms.

## Areas
- **Bedroom:** [[Mara Blackford]]'s preserved body lies here, with Ophelia seated beside it. A hairbrush held light brown, dark brown and gray hairs (see Pip's samples).
- **Kitchen:** a bubbling cauldron and a pouch with a strong **rose** scent.
- **Great hall:** enormous, with smashed doors and a gaping hole open to **daylight**.
- Several collapsed passages.

## Inhabitants
- [[Ophelia]]
- [[Mara Blackford]] (body)
