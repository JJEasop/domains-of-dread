# Gallows Crossroads

## Overview
A major crossroads north of [[Barovia]].

## Signs
- **West:** Ravenloft and [[Vallaki]]
- **South:** [[Immol]] and Stillbach
- **East:** back to [[Barovia]] and [[Sibiu]].
- **North (handwritten):** [[Tser Pool Encampment]]

## Gallows and Graveyard
A wooden platform with a two-noose gallows, and behind it a neglected cast-iron-fenced **criminal graveyard** with unmarked graves. [[Ireena Kolyana]] explained that criminals are buried here, away from town, to keep them from coming back to haunt the living.

## The Hanged Apparition
- As the party turned north, a **dead body suddenly hung** from the gallows.
- Michael's crossbow bolt embedded in it. When it spun, its face changed.
- Lucia found inconsistent postmortem signs. *Detect Magic* showed a faint aura fitting neither arcane nor divine. *Detect Evil* showed a faint evil presence.
- Lenorden and Michael each saw a specific human they knew from their past. Lucia and another companion saw **themselves** in the noose.
- [[Merrick]] advised leaving. Lucia used her Sun Domain **Greater Turning**. The apparition vanished and the embedded bolt disappeared with it.
- The party lit a candle in respect and continued north.
