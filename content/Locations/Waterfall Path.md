# Waterfall Path

## Overview
A trail beyond the [[Monolith Site]] running to a waterfall. Leads to the [[Waterfall Cave]].

- They found the body of [[Father Miroslav]] here.
- [[Merrick]] left from here to return to the [[Barovia Monastery]].
- [[Summary 6]]: [[Jordi]] confirmed the nightly ghost procession from [[Barovia]] climbs past the waterfall toward [[Castle Ravenloft]].
