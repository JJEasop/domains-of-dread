# Sanatorium

## Overview
Where the party took shelter in tight quarters after dealing with the crisis in the [[Crypt]]. They rested here in watch rotations and prepared spells.

## Events
- The party suffered unsettling dreams of **crows with human eyes** and a feeling of being stalked.
- They debated fatigue penalties, chose a shorter rest, and suspected [[Andre]], [[David]], [[Merrick]], [[Father Miroslav]] and possibly [[Ireena Kolyana]].
- After a quiet four-hour rest, they found [[Vivian]] catatonic with darting eyes.
- From here they headed toward the "whistling door": see [[Whistling Door Tunnel]].
