# Immol

> ⚠️ Spelled Immol, Emol and Imol. Confirm.

## Overview
A town south of the [[Gallows Crossroads]]. The party's planned destination for [[Ireena Kolyana]], whose sisters live there, and the safer route to [[Zidenberg]]. It has an archive, and the archivist [[Felix Meyer]] lives here. [[Sophia Kolyana]] was betrothed to its Burgermeister in 787.

## The Archive
- [[Summary 8]]: Per [[Ozrin]], the archive is **divided into sections, each needing separate permission**, plus a **public library**.
- [[Theodorus|Burgermeister Theodorus]] invites scholars to research there; Ozrin is going at his invitation.

## Events
- [[Summary 7]]: The party set out for Immol from the [[Tser Pool Encampment]]: a **3-day trip** by the [[Road to Immol]], stopping at the [[Curly Cross Inn]] and a southern village.
- [[Nastazya]] said she was taking grain to sell here.
- [[Summary 8]]: [[Ozrin]] is travelling here to research in the archive.
