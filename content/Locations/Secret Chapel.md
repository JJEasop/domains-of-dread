# Secret Chapel

## Overview
A small hidden side-chapel, 5x4 squares, reached by a short hallway behind a secret door in the [[Ancient Temple]]'s main hall. The door is sized for humans.

## Features
- Stone benches.
- A wooden **lectern** with a stone base. Its ancient lock crumbled to dust at the touch of lockpicks.

## Items Found (taken by the party)
- **Scroll** in an indecipherable language. The party plans to decipher it magically.
- **Golden disc**: one side shows a medieval-style sun with **eight rays**, the other the **full cycle of the moon's phases**.

## Events
- Sparked an in-character debate on the nature of gods, from anti-theism to deities drawing power from worshipers.
