# Ancient Temple

## Overview
A buried temple complex built for beings **taller than humans or elves**, in an architectural style like ancient Rome. Its carvings match the style of the standing stones at the [[Monolith Site]].

## Layout
- **Entry room:** round and smooth-walled, with a large columnar structure and a **railless spiral staircase** that descends about a dozen floors. The stairs have an unusually long pitch.
- **Main hall:** large and vaulted, with evenly spaced columns, a central sunken **impluvium** (rainwater basin), and a domed ceiling carved with celestial imagery.
- **Secret door** (found with a search result of 23), human-sized, leading to the [[Secret Chapel]].
- Beyond the hall: [[Ophelia's Residence]].

## Hooks
- 💡 *Suggested:* Who built it, and for whom? The tall proportions may connect to the chiseled "beast-like" figures on the standing stones.
