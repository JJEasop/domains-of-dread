# Whistling Door Tunnel

## Overview
A rough tunnel reached through the "whistling door". It exits onto the [[Cliffside Stairs]].

## Atmosphere
Wind whistles through it, with a faint lingering **scent of roses** (compare the rose perfume in the [[Crypt]]).

## Events
- The party sorted light sources and marching order before travelling through.
