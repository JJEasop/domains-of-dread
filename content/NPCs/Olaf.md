# Olaf

## Overview
A monk of the [[Barovia Monastery]]. [[Ophelia]] agreed to spare him and [[Levi]]. During the evacuation he carried [[Gertrude (Barovia Monastery Asylum)|Gertrude]] on a makeshift litter, and he reached [[Barovia]] with the caravan. He **stays behind at the [[Barovia Church]]** to help [[Alexei]] and [[Maxim]] while the rest travel on.
