# Father Miroslav

## Overview
A priest connected to the [[Barovia Monastery]]. The party found his body on the [[Waterfall Path]].

## The Body
- Dead for roughly **4–6 hours** when found.
- Terrified expression on his face.
- **No active magic detected** on the body.
- His **holy symbol is missing**. The party debated whether it was removed or ripped away.

## Timing
His estimated time of death coincides with the **ghost procession** the party witnessed. His body has since been collected and taken to the [[Barovia Monastery]].

## Theories
- Possession, or a broken spell.
- The party was suspicious of him before he died (see [[Merrick]]'s circle of suspects: [[Andre]], [[David]], Miroslav, possibly [[Ireena Kolyana]]).

## Aftermath
- [[Merrick]] returned to the monastery to manage internal threats, write a signed letter to the [[High Priestess]], and arrange retrieval of the body.
