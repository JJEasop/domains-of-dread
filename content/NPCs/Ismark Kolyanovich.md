# Ismark

> Also spelled Izmark and Esmark in the session summaries (kept as aliases).

## Overview
**The current Burgermeister of [[Barovia]].** Son of the late [[Burgermeister of Barovia]] and brother of [[Ireena Kolyana]]. He succeeded his father after the murder, before the party left Barovia.

## Events
- Left a hasty note under the church door begging for help for Ireena.
- Told the party a **tax collector** visited three days before they returned.
- Stays in Barovia to **organize the militia**. Pip and Michael helped train it.
- Gave the party gold, silver and a ring for Ireena, plus 157 gp equivalent and their mother's signet ring.
- At the funeral he was one of six pallbearers, composed and "white-knuckling" through the sudden mantle of leadership.
- Michael returned the gold chain with a "B" disc to him.

## Open Needs
- Urgently needs to find a spouse, and a household minder. The party suggested hiring [[Mary]], mother of the missing girl [[Gertrude (Missing Girl of Barovia)|Gertrude]].
