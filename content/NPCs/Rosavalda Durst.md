# Rosavalda

## Overview
Rosavalda, called **Rose**, is a **ghost** the party met in the [[Durst House]], always seen with [[Thornboldt Durst]].
Oldest child of [[Mr. Durst]] and [[Mrs. Durst]].
## Events
- The party found her body, with Thorn's, on the fourth floor.
- She and Thorn appeared to the party again and revealed that they had **starved to death**. The front door locked after that conversation.
