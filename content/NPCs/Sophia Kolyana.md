# Sophia Kolyana

## Overview
Daughter of [[Kolyan Vasilyev]]. Betrothed to the Burgermeister of [[Immol]]. She died in 787 of a "wasting illness" a few weeks before harvest, and was interred in the [[Barovia Churchyard]] near her father.

## Notes
- Her symptoms (wasting, languishing) resemble [[Ireena]]'s condition. 💡 *Suggested:* worth noting for the party's theory.
