# David

## Overview
A cleric of the [[Barovia Monastery]] held culpable, with [[Andre]], for [[Mara Blackford]]'s death. [[Ophelia]] seeks revenge on him.

## Events
- Earlier suspected by the party along with [[Andre]], [[Merrick]], [[Father Miroslav]] and possibly [[Ireena Kolyana]].
- **Defrocked** by [[Merrick]], who took his holy symbol.
- During the wolf ambush on the mountain road he **enthralled two wolves**, holding them in place with liturgical recitation.
- Being escorted to [[Zidenberg]] to face the [[High Priestess]].

## Summary 7: Road to Immol
- [[Summary 7]]: Rode in **wagon 2** with [[Merrick]]. He and [[Andre]] are now nicknamed the **"Tweedle twins."**
- After the wolf ambush on the [[Road to Immol]], went back with Pip, Andre and a horse to **retrieve [[Nastazya]]'s wagon**.
- Shared Room 2 at the [[Curly Cross Inn]] with Merrick and Andre.

## Summary 8: The Curly Cross Inn
- [[Summary 8]]: Slept in the clerics' room; found **deeply asleep and hard to rouse**, apparently **sedated**.

## Status on the Road
Travels with the party in "quasi-prisoner" status, "technically voluntary," toward [[Zidenberg]].
