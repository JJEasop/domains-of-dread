# Jordi

## Overview
The **male leader/captain** of the Vistani caravan at the [[Tser Pool Encampment]]. Brother of the caravan elder, [[Vadoma]]. 💡 *Suggested:* the unnamed elder captain with pipe and walking stick who welcomed the party in Summary 5.

## Events
- [[Summary 6]]: Greeted the party and stressed **Vistani hospitality and storytelling** tradition.
- Confirmed the nightly **ghost procession** from [[Barovia]] climbs past the waterfall toward [[Castle Ravenloft]].
- Offered a **spare vardo**, a **covered wagon** and **two draft horses**. After theatrical haggling ("Are you selling me glue?") he accepted wine, a tent, torches, a climber's kit, nails and Pip's **keg of Longbottom leaf**. He refused the silver jewelry box.
- Showed **boyish mischief** during the deal; a toddler scolded him, "Grandpa, don't be mean."
