# Ophelia

## Overview
A middle-aged woman with mousy brown hair who calls herself the **"Shadow Queen."** She is the mother of [[Mara Blackford]] and lives in the [[Ophelia's Residence]], deep beneath the [[Waterfall Cave]]. She has at least one sister, [[Granny]].

## Behavior
- Was sitting beside Mara's preserved body, singing a sad, quiet lullaby, when the party found her.
- Became irritated when asked about the failed ritual at the [[Barovia Monastery]].
- Warned the party to leave her home and **abandon the monastery**, saying **"an end is already coming"** for those responsible for her daughter's death.
- Saw through the party's *disguise self* (disguised as [[Granny]]) immediately.
- Dismissed the party after the first meeting.

## Negotiation
- Agreed to **spare the monks [[Olaf]] and [[Levi]]**.
- **Refused to release [[Gertrude (Barovia Monastery Asylum)|Gertrude]]**, a comatose girl at the monastery whom she claims she is "saving."

## Mysteries
- Pip found light brown, dark brown and gray hair in her brush, so the party suspects she is a **shape-changer**.
- A pouch in her kitchen smells strongly of **roses**, echoing the rose perfume in the [[Crypt]] and the scent in the [[Whistling Door Tunnel]].
- 💡 *Suggested:* Her home's layout and the chiseled "beast-like" monolith carvings may be linked.

## Relationships
- [[Mara Blackford]]: daughter (deceased).
- [[Granny]]: sister.
- [[Gertrude (Barovia Monastery Asylum)|Gertrude]], [[Olaf]], [[Levi]]: monastery residents she is bargaining over.
- [[Merrick]]: the party plans to report to him.

## Party Plans
Withdraw, warn the monks, and possibly return to confront her once more powerful.

## Summary 3: The Evacuation
- Her stated aim: **revenge on [[Andre]] and [[David]]**, and taking [[Gertrude (Barovia Monastery Asylum)|Gertrude]]. She allowed the party to take [[Olaf]] and [[Levi]].
- The party evacuated the [[Barovia Monastery]] and took Gertrude anyway, leaving Ophelia a **letter in forged handwriting** that explains why, says the culpable clerics are being brought to justice, and proposes a parley once Gertrude can consent. Further correspondence goes through the Bürgermeister of [[Barovia]].
- [[Mara Blackford]]'s body remains under *gentle repose*, which [[Merrick]] reads as a sign of ongoing plans. Local doctrine treats resurrection as taboo and dangerous.
- ⚠️ The summary also mentions "Ophelia's protectiveness toward Andre and David." That seems to contradict her revenge motive. Confirm.
