# Nastazya

## Overview
A woman the party rescued from a wolf attack on the [[Road to Immol]]. She says she is from [[Rottermegen]], in the north, travelling with her brother to sell grain in [[Immol]]. The party doubts her story.

## Events
- [[Summary 7]]: Defended her cart from the wolves until a bite dropped her. Lucia's *Cure Light Wounds* (11 hp) revived her, and she went into **shock** from the rapid healing.
- Her brother ([[Wilhelm]]) was dead. Her **draft horse fled**. The party walked her to the [[Curly Cross Inn]] and fetched her wagon.
- Shares **Room 3** with Lucia, [[Ireena Kolyana|Ireena]] and [[Gertrude (Barovia Monastery Asylum)|Gertrude]].

## Summary 8: Preparing the Body
- [[Summary 8]]: Prepared her brother's body for burial with [[Merrick]]'s help. Lucia joined them, partly to help and partly because the party **would not leave anyone alone with her**.

- Lucia's *Augury* on spending the night beside her alone: **Wheel and Woe** (both good and bad). Lucia (Religion 23) sensed **something was off** with the spell.
- During the night she ran to the party's door, saying **"[[Wilhelm]] is not dead."** Linnorden read her as **both panicked and calculating**. Wilhelm had risen as a ghast; Lucia destroyed him. She seemed **frightened but not deeply distressed** at losing the body.
- Watched by an invisible Linnorden, she **dropped the frightened act** once alone: confused and thoughtful, she tidied the room and **reached into the pile of dust**.
- Spotted Linnorden's attempt to make her contradict herself: "you don't need to be so suspicious." Linnorden: *"I've lived too long among humans to fully trust them all."*
- Said she **did not cause** Wilhelm's transformation, and that the party was **"more interesting than she had originally been told"**: **she was sent to watch them.**
- Then said she did not want to overstay her welcome and **walked out into the storm**. In a lightning flash she saw Michael and [[Ireena Kolyana|Ireena]], paused and **curtsied**; at the next flash she had **vanished**. [[Ozrin]] entered the yard at that moment.
- Linnorden found **a hair** on her pillow (colour not confirmed).
- Her **cheerful, helpful younger brother** seems to be a real person, but **not Wilhelm**.

## Suspicions
- Linnorden's *Detect Thoughts*: **Int 18**; her surface thoughts were **unreadable**.
- Lucia's *Detect Evil*: **ordinary (lowercase) evil**, not supernatural Evil.
- [[Merrick]]: her brother's wounds were cut **too cleanly to be from wolves**.
- Pip found a **hidden hunter's journal** in her wagon, cataloguing dangerous beasts. It describes **werewolves** as destructive magical creatures that only **pure silver** can harm.
- ⚠️ Who is she really, and how did her "brother" die?
- ⚠️ Who sent her to watch the party? Who is the real younger brother?
