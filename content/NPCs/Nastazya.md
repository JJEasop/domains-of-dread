# Nastazya

> ⚠️ Spelling from [[Summary 7]] only. Confirm.

## Overview
A woman the party rescued from a wolf attack on the [[Road to Immol]]. She says she is from [[Rottermegen]], in the north, travelling with her brother to sell grain in [[Immol]]. The party doubts her story.

## Events
- [[Summary 7]]: Defended her cart from the wolves until a bite dropped her. Lucia's *Cure Light Wounds* (11 hp) revived her, and she went into **shock** from the rapid healing.
- Her brother ([[Nastazya's Brother]]) was dead. Her **draft horse fled**. The party walked her to the [[Curly Cross Inn]] and fetched her wagon.
- Shares **Room 3** with Lucia, [[Ireena Kolyana|Ireena]] and [[Gertrude (Barovia Monastery Asylum)|Gertrude]].

## Suspicions
- Linnorden's *Detect Thoughts*: **Int 18**; her surface thoughts were **unreadable**.
- Lucia's *Detect Evil*: **ordinary (lowercase) evil**, not supernatural Evil.
- [[Merrick]]: her brother's wounds were cut **too cleanly to be from wolves**.
- Pip found a **hidden hunter's journal** in her wagon, cataloguing dangerous beasts. It describes **werewolves** as destructive magical creatures that only **pure silver** can harm.
- ⚠️ Who is she really, and how did her "brother" die?
