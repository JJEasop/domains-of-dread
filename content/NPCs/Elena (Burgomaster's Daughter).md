# Elena

## Overview
A burgomaster's daughter in the Sasha tale. Mother of [[Sasha (Monster Hunter Priest)|Sasha]].

## Events
- [[Summary 6]]: Loved the Vistani [[Petia]]; her father sent him to the gallows.
- Later married a **baker**; their son was Sasha.
- The burgomaster's home **burned in the night**, punishment from **the Count** for cheating taxes. Sasha survived.
- 💡 *Suggested:* the burgomaster was a past [[Burgermeister of Barovia]], since Petia's caravan then avoided [[Barovia]].
