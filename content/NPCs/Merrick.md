# Merrick

## Overview
**Father Merrick**, a cleric of the [[Barovia Monastery]] and the party's main ally. He asked them to investigate the [[Waterfall Cave]] while he handled matters at the monastery, and was shadowed by [[Pennig]].

## Key Actions
- Collected [[Father Miroslav]]'s body and wrote a signed letter to the [[High Priestess]].
- Met the party at the front door, took them to the library, and heard their debrief on [[Ophelia]] and [[Mara Blackford]].
- Explained local doctrine: **resurrection is taboo and feared**, historically tied to disastrous changes in the resurrected.
- **Closed the monastery temporarily** and ordered an evacuation.
- **Defrocked** [[Andre]] and [[David]] and took custody of them for judgment at [[Zidenberg]].
- Hired the party as guards and offered **500 gp and 1 sp** plus a purchased **wagon**. Payment may come as an IOU redeemable in Zidenberg, or later by runner.
- Plans to hire wagons from the nearby **Vistani camp**.

## Summary 4–5: Barovia and the Road
- Made holy water and led the restoration ritual that saved [[Ireena Kolyana]]. Afterward, exhausted, he gave the party a **large coin stamped with Zidenberg's sun** as a line of credit, to be kept safe and returned.
- Warded the church for the night, and officiated the [[Late Burgermeister of Barovia|Burgermeister of Barovia]]'s funeral because Alexei is inexperienced.
- Will place **protective wards on the wagon** carrying Ireena and [[Gertrude (Barovia Monastery Asylum)|Gertrude]].
- Travels with the party as an escort. [[Andre]] and [[David]] are "technically voluntary" prisoners.
- His deity is the **Morning Lord**, the local solar god. The party is unsure the god is truly "good."
- Believes the 787 grave in the [[Barovia Churchyard]] predates the family mausoleum.

## Summary 7: The Road to Immol
- [[Summary 7]]: Drove **wagon 2** with [[Gertrude (Barovia Monastery Asylum)|Gertrude]], [[Levi]], [[Andre]] and [[David]].
- Planned the **3-day trip** to [[Immol]] and **argued strongly against roadside camping**: night one at the [[Curly Cross Inn]], night two at a southern village.
- Cast *Bull's Strength* on Linnorden in the wolf ambush on the [[Road to Immol]].
- Noticed that [[Wilhelm|Nastazya's Brother]]'s wounds were **cut too cleanly to be from wolves**, casting doubt on [[Nastazya]].
- Shared Room 2 at the inn with [[Andre]] and [[David]].

## Summary 8: Watching Nastazya
- [[Summary 8]]: Offered to help [[Nastazya]] prepare [[Wilhelm|her brother]]'s body for burial and to **keep watch over her**. Lucia joined him.
- Shared a room with [[Andre]], [[David]] and [[Gertrude (Barovia Monastery Asylum)|Gertrude]]. After the ghast fight he was **deeply asleep and hard to rouse**, like the other clerics; Michael could not tell whether he was **drugged or just exhausted**. The [[Innkeeper (Curly Cross Inn)|innkeeper]] had **glanced toward him** before taking an item from a tray going upstairs.
- Said Gertrude is **not in immediate danger**, but someone should **stay awake with her at all times**.

## Combat Notes
- Cast *Bless*, *Prayer* and *Bear's Endurance* (on Michael) in the wolf fight.
- Killed the second large wolf with **Inflict Wounds**. The party noted this is a dark choice for a cleric of justice.
