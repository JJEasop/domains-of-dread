# Hawthorne

## Overview
An ally Pip wrote to for help with [[Ireena Kolyana]], sent by the raven [[Pennig]]. Hawthorne replied she cannot directly assist now and told the party to **travel south rather than north** and seek her **sisters in Emol or Velaki**. Emol is safer but slower.

Hawthorne can see the weave. Powerful fae can see the weave. 

Rescued Party from a group of Will-o-Whisps. Informed the Party the [[Gertrude (Missing Girl of Barovia)]] is safe, and she does not want to be found. 

The Sisterhood takes in people of power and people of need. They try protect the land.
