# Ozrin

## Overview
A **scholarly noble** from [[Vallaki]], of a family with a **long academic history**. Met in a corner of the [[Curly Cross Inn]], surrounded by research papers and scrolls, with an [[Scholar's Bodyguard (Curly Cross Inn)|armed bodyguard]]. **Speaks Draconic**, which he calls **"the Halan"**. Travelling to [[Immol]] to research in its archive at the invitation of [[Theodorus|Burgermeister Theodorus]].

## Events
- [[Summary 7]]: Linnorden's *Detect Thoughts* read **Int 17**, but he **passed his save** and his thoughts stayed hidden.
- Lucia's *Detect Evil*: **ordinary (lowercase) evil**.

## Summary 8: Conversation in Draconic
- [[Summary 8]]: Linnorden, with Pip, opened in **Draconic**. Ozrin was **delighted** to meet someone speaking what he recognized as the language and asked where they learned it.
- Linnorden told him they came from the [[Forest of Arantellion]] and arrived in Barovia lost in the mists. Ozrin recognized them as **"Mist Walkers"** and eagerly asked questions.
- Said he is from [[Vallaki]] and travelling to [[Immol]] at the invitation of [[Theodorus|Burgermeister Theodorus]] to research in the archive.
- The archive is **divided into sections, each needing separate permission**. He had only used its **public library** before; this is his **first invitation** to research there, and he is enthusiastic about it.
- Traded spells with Linnorden: Ozrin had little to offer and agreed to **copy *Mage Armor***.
- Said violence and warnings against going out after dark are ordinary in Barovia, but called **werewolf stories possibly nonsense**. Linnorden's Sense Motive: **he was lying** about that.
- On Linnorden's **waystone sketch**: he has seen one **north of [[Vallaki]], across [[Lake Zarovich]]**, but cannot read it or tell its purpose. Suggested it might interest [[Theodorus]] and win Linnorden archive access.
- Considered **bypassing [[Stillbrook]]** because of the missing people; his [[Scholar's Bodyguard (Curly Cross Inn)|bodyguard]] looked unhappy at the idea of camping in the wilderness.
- Linnorden offered to ask the party whether he could **travel with them**. **No decision yet.**
- Pip answered his academic name-dropping with his own family history and the ***There and Back Again* folio**.
- **Late that night**, as [[Nastazya]] vanished into the storm, Ozrin **came into the inn yard from the road** and looked toward where she had been. He remarked that the party was **"supposed to be asleep."** The session ended there.
