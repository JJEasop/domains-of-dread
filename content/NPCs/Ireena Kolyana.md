# Ireena

> Also spelled Irina in some session summaries (kept as an alias).

## Overview
The **daughter of the [[Burgermeister of Barovia]]** and sister of [[Ismark Kolyanovich]]. A vampire bit her twice. Per local lore she had to avoid a third bite across **three consecutive nights**. The third night passed unbitten, her colour returned, and she is recovering, though tired, grieving and disoriented without her home.

## Vampire Attacks
- Two small puncture marks on her neck, identified by Linnorden. See [[The Vampire]].
- Moved to the [[Barovia Church]] under wards, where [[Merrick]] performed a restoration ritual.
- The vampire searched her room after failing to breach the church.
- Her father was killed while writing to the Burgermeister of [[Vallaki]] asking protection for her.

## Belongings
- Her sword, armor and clothes.
- From [[Ismark Kolyanovich]]: about **157 gp equivalent (mostly platinum)** and her **mother's wedding signet ring**. She can now hold her own funds and belongings.

## Journey
Escorted with [[Gertrude (Barovia Monastery Asylum)|Gertrude]], [[Merrick]], [[Andre]] and [[David]], south toward her **sisters in [[Immol]]** ([[Hawthorne]]'s advice: south is safer than north). Lucia likely rides with her. [[Merrick]] will ward the wagon. Currently at the [[Tser Pool Encampment]] to buy a wagon.

## Tarokka Reading
- [[Summary 6]]: [[Vadoma]] read for her at the [[Tser Pool Encampment]]. Her card: **Eight of Pentacles (The Necromancer)**: "a woman hanging over a roaring fire. Find her, and you will find what you seek." See [[Tarokka Reading (Summary 6)]].

## Summary 7: On the Road to Immol
- [[Summary 7]]: Showed **complete physical recovery**. Drove **wagon 1** on the [[Road to Immol]], with Lucia and Michael in the front cab.
- In the wolf ambush she **leapt from the wagon** and killed the first wolf with a **critical rapier strike (16 damage)**.
- Spent the night at the [[Curly Cross Inn]] in Room 3 with Lucia, [[Gertrude (Barovia Monastery Asylum)|Gertrude]] and [[Nastazya]].

## Summary 8: Night at the Inn
- [[Summary 8]]: The [[Innkeeper's Wife (Curly Cross Inn)|innkeeper's wife]] gave her and Michael **drugged wine** with a note, **"Do not drink"**. They didn't.
- Shared a room with Lucia, Michael, Pip and Linnorden. Was **already awake and armed** when [[Nastazya]] raised the alarm.
- Checked the perimeter and the [[Stable Boy (Curly Cross Inn)|stable boy]] with Michael, and overheard the innkeepers arguing about "it" and "the children".
- Saw Nastazya **curtsy** in a lightning flash and then vanish.
