# Doctor Donovich

## Overview
The physician of [[Barovia]]. He sold the party three *cure light wounds* potions (50 gp each). Lucia visited to thank him and ask about vampirism. He was **visibly nervous** (spilled tea, shaking hands) when the topic came up.

## Views on Vampirism
- More like a **transmissible curse** than a disease.
- Animal blood can "wet the appetite" but does not sustain a vampire.
- A vampire who controls the hunger can be good or evil as in life. When the hunger controls them, they are beasts.
- Knows **no cure other than death**, but researches cures and asked to be told of any medical findings.
