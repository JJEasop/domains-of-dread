# Malocchio

## Overview
The **Dukkar** of Vistani legend: a male Vistani born with **the sight** (unusual and forbidden for a boy), prophesied enemy of the Vistani. Son of [[Gabriella (Vistani Legend)|Gabriella]].

## Events
- [[Summary 6]]: Trained by his mother to hunt Vistani. **Killed her first**, then hunted the Vistani relentlessly.
