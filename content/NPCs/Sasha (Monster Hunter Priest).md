# Sasha

## Overview
Hero of a Vistani tale told by [[Marcella (Vistani Storyteller)|Marcella]]. Son of [[Elena (Burgomaster's Daughter)|Elena]] and a baker. Became a priest who teaches others to **hunt the undead**.

## Events
- [[Summary 6]]: Survived the fire that burned the burgomaster's home.
- Raised by the priest [[Father Martin]], who taught him to hunt creatures of the night.
- With his friend [[Liesel]] and the [[Golden-Faced Elf]], tried to infiltrate **the Lord's castle** (💡 *Suggested:* [[Castle Ravenloft]]). Found **treasures in the crypt** to fight the dark creatures.
- Thought he had killed them, but **later learned he had not**.
