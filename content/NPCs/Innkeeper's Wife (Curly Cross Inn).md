# Innkeeper's Wife (Curly Cross Inn)

## Overview
Wife of the [[Innkeeper (Curly Cross Inn)|innkeeper]] at the [[Curly Cross Inn]]. She **secretly warned the party** that their wine was drugged. Name unknown.

## Events
- [[Summary 8]]: Delivered drinks to Michael and [[Ireena Kolyana|Ireena]] with a small note: **"Do not drink."** The dark red wine held a **sedative** (a "bitter plant" smell).
- Served the food separately, making **pointed eye contact** with Michael and nodding encouragingly. The party took it to mean the food was safe and ate.
- Overheard by Michael and Ireena arguing quietly with the innkeeper in the kitchen: she **could not, in good conscience, let "it" happen**. He told her to **think about the children**, and she wept.

- ⚠️ What is "it", and whose children? 💡 *Suggested:* someone may be forcing the innkeepers' hand by threatening their children.
