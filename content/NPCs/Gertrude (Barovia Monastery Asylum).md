# Gertrude

## Overview
A **comatose girl** who was kept in the asylum at the [[Barovia Monastery]]. She is **not** related to [[Mary]]'s missing daughter, who is also named Gertrude (see [[Gertrude (Missing Girl of Barovia)]]). [[Ophelia]] claims she is "saving" her and warned the party not to touch her. The party took her anyway, out of concern for her agency and safety.

## Events
- Carried by [[Olaf]] on a makeshift litter (ladder and mattress) during the evacuation.
- The party left Ophelia a letter explaining why, and proposing a parley once Gertrude can consent.
- Now travels with the party. [[Merrick]] will ward her wagon, and Lucia plans to consecrate it daily.

## Concerns
- The party fears Ophelia's claimed "cure" could be worse than Gertrude's condition. They also speculate that taking her may have contributed to the wolf attack.
- 💡 *Suggested:* Compare her condition with [[Vivian]], who is catatonic at the [[Sanatorium]].
