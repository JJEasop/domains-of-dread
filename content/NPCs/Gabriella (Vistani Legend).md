# Gabriella

## Overview
A figure from a cautionary Vistani tale told by [[Marcella (Vistani Storyteller)|Marcella]]. Mother of [[Malocchio]], the Dukkar.

## Events
- [[Summary 6]]: Daughter of a proud woman with the sight, once promised as **Rauni**. Her mother warned her **not to bear children**.
- Gathering herbs under a full moon, she **tripped her own mother** to escape a beast. The Mists rejected her selfishness and put her in the realm of a **Lord who hunted young brides monthly**.
- She **froze the Lord with her gaze**, killed him with a **silver dagger**, took his palace and became **mortu**.
- Bore [[Malocchio]] and trained him to hunt Vistani. He killed her first.
