# Walter

## Overview
Illegitimate child of [[Mr. Durst]] and the family maid [[Margret]]
A "baby" found on the altar of the sacrificial chamber at the bottom of the [[Durst House]] basement, ready to be sacrificed. Walter was **not living**: made of human material but not a human baby, **flesh reconstituted into the shape of a baby**.

## Relationships
- [[Margret]]: begged the party to save him.
