# Wilhelm

> Named in [[Summary 8]]. Previously "Nastazya's Brother" (kept as an alias).

## Overview
A man found **dead on the ground** beside [[Nastazya]]'s cart during the wolf attack on the [[Road to Immol]]. Nastazya called him her brother. That night at the [[Curly Cross Inn]] he **rose as a ghast** and Lucia destroyed him.

## Events
- [[Summary 7]]: Lucia confirmed him dead (Medicine 18). The party loaded his body onto Nastazya's wagon, which Pip, [[Andre]] and [[David]] brought to the [[Curly Cross Inn]].
- [[Merrick]] noted his wounds were **cut far too cleanly** for wolf claws or teeth.
- ⚠️ The summary puts "brother" in quotes. Was he really her brother?

## Summary 8: Risen as a Ghast
- [[Summary 8]]: [[Nastazya]] and [[Merrick]] prepared his body for burial, with Lucia present. Lucia had considered *Gentle Repose* to preserve him for the **~week-long journey to [[Rottermegen]]**.
- During the night Nastazya ran to the party's door, saying **"Wilhelm is not dead."** In the room they found him **risen as a ghast**, like the undead in the [[Durst House]] but with an **overwhelming stench**.
- Lucia's Sun Domain **Greater Turning** (16 + cleric level = 22) **destroyed him; he turned to dust**.
- Lucia and Linnorden gathered the dust into a **waterskin** with *Mage Hand*. Nastazya said the remains would now be "easier to transport". Where they will be taken is undecided.
- Nastazya said she **did not cause** his transformation.
- Linnorden concluded that the cheerful, helpful **younger brother** Nastazya described is a real person, but **not Wilhelm**.
- ⚠️ Who has the waterskin now that Nastazya has left?
