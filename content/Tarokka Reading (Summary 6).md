# Tarokka Reading (Summary 6)

Given by [[Vadoma]] at the [[Tser Pool Encampment]] in [[Summary 6]], to the four PCs and [[Ireena Kolyana|Ireena]]. Vadoma would not interpret it.

## Individual Draws
| Who | Card | Reading |
|---|---|---|
| Ireena | Eight of Pentacles (The Necromancer) | A woman hanging over a roaring fire. Find her, and you will find what you seek. |
| Michael | Eight of Coins (The Tax Collector) | "My people have what you seek." A missing child holds the key. |
| Pip | Five of Coins (The Guild Member) | A dark room full of bottles: the tomb of a guild member. Treasure there. |
| Lucia | Six of Swords (The Sorcerer) | Find the mad dog's crypt. Treasure beneath blackened bones. |
| Linnorden | The Tempter | Another child, perhaps the same. Hurry. **Do not lose her in the water.** (A warning-command.) |

## Group Cross
| Position | Card | Reading |
|---|---|---|
| Past | Six of Glyphs (The Anarchist) | Bones on wall, chandelier and table: remains of long-forgotten enemies. |
| Present | Seven of Pentacles (The Illusionist) | A man not what he seems comes in a wagon, like and unlike a vardo. Great treasure therein. |
| Future | Eight of Glyphs (The Bishop) | A secret in a pile of treasure beyond golden doors. |
| Treachery | Six of Pentacles (The Evoker) | The crypts of a great wizard. His staff is the key. |
| Fate | Two of Pentacles (The Diviner) | The one who sees all. She holds the key to your fate. |

## Companions
- **Five of Pentacles (The Elementalist):** someone who knows a place "where sickness and madness are bred, where children once cried," and will lead the party there.
- **The Innocent.**

All readings are tracked as open questions in the [[Quest Log]].
